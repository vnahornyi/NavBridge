# Reports

No executed NavBridge harness or device reports exist yet. Local raw reports belong to ignored `local/`; only reviewed redacted summaries may be tracked here later.

A report records task/scenario, revision, actual command, toolchain/device environment, configuration, provenance/reference, completion status, phase/effect trace summary, metrics with units and uncertainty, failures, skipped checks, and limitations. Distinguish not run, pass, fail, blocked, and inconclusive.

Never claim field accuracy from synthetic replay or background support from a simulator pass. Do not include pairing keys, provisioning data, raw device identifiers, or private routes in a public report.
